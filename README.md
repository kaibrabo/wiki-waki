# Moondial

**Your alarms, pinned to the places that matter.**

Moondial is an alarm app for people who live across timezones. Set an alarm for "9am in San Francisco" and it rings at the right moment wherever you are - whether you're home, in Tokyo, or somewhere over the Pacific.

---

## The Problem

Your phone's alarm app doesn't understand timezones. Set an alarm for 9am, fly to another country, and it fires at the wrong time. You're left doing mental math, juggling world clocks, and missing meetings.

Remote workers, frequent travelers, and globally distributed teams deal with this every day.

## The Solution

Moondial pins each alarm to a specific timezone. Your "Start work" alarm stays anchored to San Francisco time. Your "Call with Tokyo office" alarm stays anchored to Tokyo time. They fire at the correct instant, automatically adjusting for where you physically are.

No math. No mistakes. Just alarms that work the way you think.

---

## How It Works

1. **Add locations** - San Francisco, Tokyo, London, wherever you operate
2. **Create alarms for each location** - "9am SF standup", "6pm Tokyo sync"
3. **Travel freely** - alarms fire at the right moment, no matter where you are

Each location shows as a card with the current local time, upcoming alarms, and a quick status indicator. Tap a card to manage its alarms.

---

## Features

- **Timezone-pinned alarms** - set once, works everywhere
- **Location cards** - weather-app style interface showing time across your world
- **DST handled automatically** - uses IANA timezone database, not fixed offsets
- **Works offline** - local-first, no account required
- **Dark mode** - easy on the eyes at 3am

## Description (4000)

Waimea is an alarm app for people who live across time zones.

Every alarm you create is anchored to a place and its time zone. Set a 9:00 AM alarm in San Francisco and it will ring at San Francisco's 9:00 AM, whether you are home, in Tokyo, or on a red-eye somewhere over the Pacific. No more mental math, no more alarms that drift when you cross a border.

WHY Waimea

Regular alarm apps fire at whatever the clock on your phone says. That breaks the moment you travel: your "wake at 7" becomes 7 in the wrong zone. Waimea fixes this by pinning each alarm to the time zone you meant, so your morning routine, your medication reminders, and your meeting nudges all stay put.

FEATURES

- Timezone-anchored alarms. Each alarm carries its own time zone and rings at that exact local time, anywhere in the world.
- Location cards. See your saved places as clean cards with live local times, like a world clock that also holds your alarms.
- Real ringing alarms. On supported devices, alarms ring loudly with a full-screen Stop and Snooze, even through silent mode and Focus. On other devices they arrive as notifications.
- Per-alarm sounds. Choose from several distinct tones, including a classic bugle Reveille.
- Flexible repeats. Daily, weekdays, custom days, monthly, or one-time.
- Home Screen widgets. Small, medium, and large widgets show your current time and location, your next alarm with a live countdown, and world clocks for your saved places.
- Apple Watch app and complications. Glance at your next alarm and its countdown right from your wrist or your watch face.
- Made for the world. 12 languages, 12 or 24-hour time, MM/DD or DD/MM dates, and light or dark themes.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Waimea keeps your data on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. Location is used only to detect your current time zone, and it never leaves your phone and paired Apple Watch. See our privacy policy for details.

Set it once, travel anywhere, and wake up on time.
